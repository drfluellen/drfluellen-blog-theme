## The name

*Occasions and Causes* comes from Fluellen, the Welsh captain in Shakespeare's *Henry V*. Fluellen is pedantic, loyal, and devoted to the ancient disciplines of the wars.{{< sidenote >}}In 5.1 he explains to Gower why he is still wearing a leek the day after St. David's Day. The reason is Pistol, and the leek is about to be eaten.{{< /sidenote >}} He is also sure that everything has its reasons, if you're patient enough to find them. That seemed like a fair motto for a blog about theology, teaching, and technology. It's also where the @drfluellen handle comes from.

## Typography

The text is set in **ET Book**, the typeface Dmitry Krasny, Bonnie Scranton, and Edward Tufte designed for Tufte's books on information design. It's modeled on the Renaissance roman type cut for Aldus Manutius in Venice.{{< marginnote >}}The same family of type as Monotype Bembo, named for Pietro Bembo, whose *De Aetna* (1495–96) was the first book set in it.{{< /marginnote >}} Notes sit in the margin beside the text rather than at the foot of the page, as in Tufte's books.

## Design

The theme is adapted from **Tufte for Micro.blog** by Pete Moore. It was ported from SlashForMotion's *hugo-tufte*, which is built on Dave Liepmann's *Tufte CSS*. I've changed the header, updated the templates for Hugo 0.158, and added a few small things. All of the underlying work is under the MIT License and used with thanks. The social icons come from Simple Icons, which are in the public domain.

## Publishing

The blog is written in Markdown and hosted on [Micro.blog](https://micro.blog), which builds the site with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io). Posts are also cross-posted to Threads. You can follow by [RSS](/feed.xml), by [JSON Feed](/feed.json), or on Micro.blog.

## Privacy

Visitor numbers are counted with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), which uses no cookies and doesn't track individual readers. No advertising, and no tracking by Google or Meta.
